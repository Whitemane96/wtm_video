WTM Video for Mac
=================

1. Drag "WTM Video" into your Applications folder.
2. Open it. The first time, your Mac will probably say it can't check the app
   for malicious software. That is normal for apps that don't come from the
   App Store, and it's safe to continue:

   - Click "Done" (not "Move to Trash").
   - Open  System Settings  >  Privacy & Security.
   - Scroll down to the message about "WTM Video" and click "Open Anyway".
   - Enter your password, then click "Open".

   You only have to do this once.

   (On older versions of macOS you can instead right-click the app and choose
   Open, then click Open again.)

3. The first time it starts, WTM Video downloads a couple of helper programs.
   It needs an internet connection and takes a minute. After that it opens
   straight away.

Please only download things you have the right to download.

If the steps above don't work, open the Terminal app and paste this line, then
try opening the app again:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/WTM Video.app"
