WTM Video for Windows
=====================

1. Right-click the zip file and choose "Extract All", then open the folder.
   (Please extract it first. Running the program from inside the zip won't work.)
2. Double-click "WTM Video.exe".
3. The first time, Windows will probably show a blue "Windows protected your
   PC" window. That is normal for apps that don't come from a big company or
   the Microsoft Store, and it's safe to continue:

   - Click "More info".
   - Click "Run anyway".

   You only have to do this once.

4. The first time it starts, WTM Video downloads a couple of helper programs.
   It needs an internet connection and takes a minute. After that it opens
   straight away.

You can move the folder anywhere you like. To remove the app, just delete it.
The helper programs are stored in  %LOCALAPPDATA%\wtm_video  if you want to
clear those too.

Please only download things you have the right to download.
